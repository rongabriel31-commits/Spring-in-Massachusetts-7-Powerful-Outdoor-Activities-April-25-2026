Overview
Spring in Massachusetts marks the return of full outdoor access across state-managed lands. As temperatures rise, the Department of Conservation and Recreation (DCR) resumes seasonal programming across parks, reservations, and coastal systems.
This transition is visible at locations such as Blue Hills Reservation, Charles River Reservation, and Belle Isle Marsh, where trail access improves and wildlife activity increases.
Outdoor activity is distributed across environments rather than centralized, allowing visitors to explore forests, wetlands, reservoirs, and coastal areas.
